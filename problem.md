# MWALIMU RULE-BASED INTELLINGENCE LEARNING SYSTEM
## PYTHON BASED ADAPTIVE LEARNING SYSTEM FOR JUNIOR LEARNERS
Mwalimu-RBI  is a command-line education assistant for junior learners to aid them in performing basic mathematical calculations and also enhance their literacy in English and Swahili languages.
### PROBLEM STATEMENT
In early stages of education learners find it difficult to learn due to their different levels of understandings.At times learners who have difficulties in understanding concepts are left behind and this problem affects them in later stages of education.Mwalimu-RBI aids in bridging that gap between "slow learners" and academia.
### TARGET USERS
The primary users include:
-Learners
The secondary users include:
-Teachers
-Parents

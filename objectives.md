# MWALIMU RULE BASED INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM
## Objectives
The main objectives of the learning system is to enable learners:
1. Perform basic mathematical calculations and have understanding of basic mathematical calculations.
2. Read and write English and Swahili words with little or no difficulty.
3. Spell words with little difficulty.
The system should also be able to:
1. Pin point a learners area of strength and weakness.
2. Record performance.
3. Change question difficulty.
4. Provide Feedback.
5. Adapt to changes.
